Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Iron Digital.

How long does a standard website build take?
Most standard website builds are completed within 1 to 2 weeks, depending on the scope of the project and how quickly we can finalize your content and media.
Who owns the website and the design?
You fully own your website and your domain name. However, Iron Digital retains the rights to our custom design structures unless you have provided an original, exact template (not a screenshot of an existing site) that we followed perfectly.
What happens if I need changes after the site is live?
Our Starter Build includes a 7-day window for post-launch adjustments. Additionally, all websites are protected by our Emergency Maintenance Warranty. This covers broken links, website crashes, and emergency takedowns for 6 months after your last subscription payment, or 3 months after a standard plan payment.
Do you only work with businesses in Detroit?
Not at all. While we are incredibly proud of our Detroit roots and local hustle, we build digital ecosystems for businesses, brands, and individuals worldwide, including international clients.
