Frequently asked questions regarding integration and deployment.

How fast can we deploy a new cluster?
For Standard and Scale tiers, automated provisioning takes less than 5 minutes. Enterprise Matrix deployments require a brief consultation to map your architecture but are usually live within 24 hours.
Who owns the data passing through the network?
You retain 100% ownership and IP rights. NovaTech operates strictly as a zero-knowledge infrastructure provider. We do not use your data to train our own internal models.
What is your uptime guarantee?
We offer an industry-leading 99.999% uptime SLA for Enterprise and Quantum tiers, backed by financially significant credits for any downtime.
Do you only host companies in Austin, Texas?
No. While our primary data centers and headquarters are located in Austin, we operate Edge compute nodes globally and serve multinational enterprise clients across all continents.
